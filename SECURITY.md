# Security

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue: on GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Useful to include: what an attacker could do, the steps to reproduce it, and the Couchgram version.

Things in scope include anything that could leak a Telegram session, let another app or device control Couchgram, or make it post to a channel without the phone remote being on.
